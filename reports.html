Data & Interpretation

Reports use saved invoice records. Product-level analysis requires item names, quantities, unit prices and category details. Hourly analysis requires valid invoice timestamps. Cancelled invoices should be excluded from valid sales. Revenue is not net profit; profit analysis requires product costs and operating expenses.
