Data & Interpretation

Sales revenue represents invoice sales, not net profit. Profit analysis requires reliable product costs and operating expenses. Product-level insights require item names, quantities and prices to be saved with each invoice. Hourly insights require accurate transaction timestamps. Cancelled invoices must be excluded from valid sales totals.
